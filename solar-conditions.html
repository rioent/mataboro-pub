Reading the dial: Solar Flux Index (SFI) tracks 10.7 cm solar radio emission and is the single best proxy for how strongly the ionosphere is ionizing. Higher SFI means more F2‑layer ionization — better refraction of higher HF bands (15m/12m/10m) and longer skip distances.
